<Definition term={"Deep Learning"}>
Deep learning is a subfield of machine learning that uses neural networks with many learned layers to transform raw or weakly structured inputs into useful representations. It underpins modern language, vision, speech and multimodal systems, and requires practical understanding of architectures, training objectives, optimisation, regularisation, data scale, hardware acceleration and failure modes such as overfitting or spurious correlation.
</Definition>

## Why It Matters

Deep learning uses multilayer neural networks. It underpins modern vision, speech, multimodal systems and large language models.

Important concepts include tensors, embeddings, architectures, training, inference, optimisation, transfer learning and compute requirements. For AI engineers, deep learning matters because model capabilities and constraints shape product design, evaluation cost and deployment choices.
