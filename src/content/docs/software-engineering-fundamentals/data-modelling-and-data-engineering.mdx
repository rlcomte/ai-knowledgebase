<Definition term={"Data Modelling and Data Engineering"}>
Data modelling defines the entities, relationships, events, identifiers, semantics and lifecycle states that software relies on. Data engineering builds the pipelines, storage, validation, lineage and access patterns that make data usable. AI systems need both: models can only reason well over data that is coherent, current, governed and connected to the business meaning of the task.
</Definition>

## Why It Matters

Data modelling represents entities, relations, events, constraints, lineage and lifecycle. AI does not compensate for ambiguous semantics or poor-quality source data.

For AI applications, data engineering also includes provenance, freshness, retention and access policies for retrieved context, traces, feedback and eval datasets.
