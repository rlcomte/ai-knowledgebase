<Definition term={"Requirements and Design"}>
Requirements and design turn an ambiguous need into a precise description of users, workflows, constraints, system boundaries and acceptance conditions. For AI-supported systems this includes deciding which behaviour must be deterministic, which uncertainty is acceptable, what data is needed, how errors will be detected and how the user experience makes model limitations visible.
</Definition>

## Why It Matters

Requirements engineering elicits, analyses, specifies, validates and manages functional and quality requirements. AI features additionally require behavioural examples, uncertainty policies and escalation rules.

Software design divides responsibilities among components, defines interfaces and makes trade-offs explicit. Good design limits the scope of AI uncertainty and keeps deterministic rules deterministic.

Computational thinking and programming fundamentals give engineers the vocabulary for decomposition, abstraction, algorithms, state, complexity and failure.
