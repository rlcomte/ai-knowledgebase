## Before Implementation

Answer these questions before committing to a full build.

<div class="canvas-grid">
<section><h2>Whose situation should improve?</h2><p>Stakeholder and user description</p></section>
<section><h2>What happens today?</h2><p>Current workflow and pain points</p></section>
<section><h2>What outcome matters?</h2><p>Product hypothesis and outcome metric</p></section>
<section><h2>Why might AI help?</h2><p>AI-suitability argument</p></section>
<section><h2>Where should AI not decide?</h2><p>Deterministic boundaries and human authority</p></section>
<section><h2>What data and context are required?</h2><p>Data, grounding and provenance design</p></section>
<section><h2>What can go wrong?</h2><p>Failure modes and risk controls</p></section>
<section><h2>What does good look like?</h2><p>Acceptance criteria and eval design</p></section>
<section><h2>What is the smallest end-to-end test?</h2><p>Prototype or vertical slice</p></section>
<section><h2>What evidence permits release?</h2><p>Quality thresholds and accountable decision owner</p></section>
</div>
