## Why It Matters

Value, feasibility, viability and responsibility are four complementary tests: does it help users, can it be built, can it be sustained and should it be deployed under the applicable values and risks?

These tests keep teams from over-optimising model performance while ignoring adoption, operational cost, organisational constraints or harm.
