Read an area overview first when learning a topic from scratch. Use concept pages for quick reference. Follow related-concept links when a decision crosses boundaries between product shaping, AI behaviour, software quality and agent-assisted implementation.

Each concept page follows a compact template: definition, why it matters, engineering mode, evaluation check, real example, related concepts and source note. Short concepts stay concise rather than being padded with generic prose.



<SourceNote>
The site's organising structure follows Andrew Ng's <a href="https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/the-ai-engineering-skills-map/">AI Engineering Skills Map</a>, published by DeepLearning.AI in <em>The Batch</em>. Ng's map is the primary source for the four areas. Area 1 follows Ng's more detailed writing; Areas 2-4 are this site's reasoned extension.
</SourceNote>
